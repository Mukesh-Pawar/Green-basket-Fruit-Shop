About Our Company

Green Basket is a community-driven online fruit store created by a group of dedicated local farmers with one simple mission—to deliver fresh, naturally grown fruits directly from farms to your doorstep. By working together, our farming partners ensure that every fruit is carefully selected, hygienically packed, and delivered with the highest quality standards. Our efficient order management and local delivery network help us provide same-day delivery for most orders, ensuring that you enjoy the freshness of farm-picked fruits without unnecessary delays. At Green Basket, every order supports hardworking farmers while bringing healthy, delicious, and fresh produce to your family.
